B separated mark — not adopted
fspark9
Wordmark stays complete and typographic; the mark lives alone for avatar, favicon and stamp. The two are never set on the same line — that is what caused the stutter.

02The symbol — the closing slice
The ring is closed. Navy carries 304° of it; bronze carries the last 64°, centred exactly on the apex — the point where the bowl comes round to close. The slice is a true segment of the ring: identical outer circle, identical counter, cut on two clean radial edges. Only the colour changes, so nothing bulges and nothing is notched — it reads as the final piece seated flush, not as a wedge laid over the drawing.
The figure now sits inside the same box as the letters: ring top on cap height, tail terminal on the baseline, no descender. Counter reduced so the bowl's optical weight matches the s, p and a of Playfair Bold — 15.5 units on the flanks, 12.5 at top and bottom.
