# fspark9 Site Copy v1

September 2026. Based on Positioning v5 and Brand Book v2. English first, Turkish after each block. Turkish is written in Turkish, never translated line by line.

**Tone:** warm, direct and short. Mehmet speaks in the first person. "We" means Mehmet and the client's team working together. No dashes, no "X, not Y", no filler words.

**Unchanged:** the four legal pages (Imprint, Privacy, Cookies, Terms of use) keep the exact text of the current site, word for word, in both languages. Only the design changes. They share one template with a tab strip on top. The footer links only to Imprint, and the other three are reached from the tabs on the legal page. Privacy and Cookies get the updates in fspark9-legal-update-v2.md. Spark and The Last Day have new, shorter copy (sections 5 and 6).

**Subpages:** every subpage opens with a small back link ("← Home", "← Work", "← Spark") and ends with the same Next step block as the home page. The label reads "Next step" (TR: "Sonraki adım") with no number.

**One call to action everywhere:** every booking button on the site reads "Book a call" (TR: "Görüşme ayarla"). The header button, the opening, Next step, service pages and About all use the same text. The button never leaves the site: it opens our own booking window with the cal.com embed inside (event: mburakdikmen/quick-chat). Window title: "Book a call · Free · 30 minutes" (TR: "Görüşme ayarla · Ücretsiz · 30 dakika"). Desktop shows it as a centred window over the page, mobile as a full screen sheet.

## 3.2 insha

**EN**
**insha**
Europe's first interest free, digital only bank. · *Zero to Live*

**Where they were stuck**
Albaraka Türk wanted to serve Europe's Muslim community. All it had was the intention. No product, no infrastructure, no European partner and no local trust.

**What we did**
- Defined what the product was and who would pay for it, before any code was written.
- Chose and negotiated the BaaS and card partners, and put the product under a BaFin licensed institution.
- Designed the whole digital experience, including an account people could open on their phone in one sitting, with no paperwork.
- Built growth on the ground, because a bank nobody has heard of can't buy trust with ads.

**What happened**
Europe's first interest free digital only bank went live in Germany, on solarisBank with a Mastercard debit card. It went live six months after we started. Users grew more than 300% through 2020 to over 40,000.
