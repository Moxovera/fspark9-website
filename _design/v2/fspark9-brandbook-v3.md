# fspark9 Brand Book v2 · The Ninth Slice

September 2026. Approved. Replaces Fspark9_Brandbook.md (v1: navy, bronze, Fraunces). Visual version: https://claude.ai/artifact/VFbvSy1PLW5PkvttC5R3ty

## 1. Idea
A launch has nine slices, from the first proposition to the day it goes live. Most teams get through eight. fspark9 closes the ninth. That slice is the only bright colour in the system.
Name stays fspark9. Mehmet is the face. Model: a firm with a face.

## 2. Logo (C2, one body with one slice, approved September 2026)
- Wordmark: "fspark" in Epilogue 800, tracking -3.5%, outlined in the files, followed by a drawn 9. The 9 is cap height and sits on the baseline, 0.07em after the k. Never type the 9.
- The 9 is one body: ring and tail joined, no gaps. Only the ninth slice (320 to 360 degrees, closing at twelve o'clock) is cut out with two thin parallel cuts and set in Flare.
- One drawing at every size, from favicon to billboard. No small or simplified variant.
- Versions: Ink on Paper/White (primary), Paper on Ink (reversed), one colour, symbol alone, favicon, app icon, LinkedIn avatar.
- Clear space: height of the ring of the 9 on every side. Minimum: wordmark 96 px / 22 mm, below that the symbol alone.
- Never: recolour the slice, add more slices, move it from twelve, place on Flare, Stone or a raw photo, add effects, capital F, stretch, rebuild in another font.
- Files: fspark9-logo-v2.zip (svg/, png/, favicon.ico). Geometry: symbol viewBox `18 0 64 100`, ring centre (50,32), outer radius 32, inner 17, cut width 2.8.
- Any change to the mark needs Mehmet's sign off.

## 5. The slice (signature, three uses only)
- Dial: nine slices. The ninth slice is the spark and is always Flare, on every dial and for every service, whatever else is filled. fspark9 brings that spark to every piece of work, whole launch or single slice. The other eight are filled in Ink (Paper on an Ink ground) where the work covers them and left empty in Rule where it does not. On service rows the dial shows the slices a service covers, with the Flare slice always on. The slice map is fixed: 1 Strategy, 2 Business model and first product, 3 Compliance bridge, 4 Partners, 5 and 6 Product and experience, 7 and 8 Go to market and launch, 9 the first product live. Product & Strategy lights 1, 2, 4, 5, 6. Embedded Finance lights 2, 4, 5, 6. Expansion & GTM lights 3, 4, 7, 8. Zero to Live lights all. Partners is lit in every service.
- Hero ring: one large outline ring on the Ink opening. Eight slices drawn as thin lines, only the ninth filled in Flare. The portrait is a circle that sits exactly inside the ring's inner hole, sharing its centre, with a thin even gap of Ink between photo and ring (desktop: ring 700 px, portrait 466 px; mobile: ring 420 px, portrait 278 px). The whole ring, Flare slice included, stays visible. Once per page, opening only.
- Service dial (home, Four services): a large dial next to the service rows. Resting state shows the whole ring. Hovering or focusing a row fills only that service's slices and dims the other rows. The ninth slice stays Flare throughout.
- Cut: Flare block with slanted right edge. `clip-path: polygon(0 0,100% 0,calc(100% - .32em) 100%,0 100%)`. Button: same with 18px.
- Marker block: the cut highlight at section scale. One Flare block with a slanted right edge that runs in from the left edge of the page, holding the proof figures in Ink with 1 px Ink dividers. Used for the proof figures on case pages. Replaces the beam there.
- Beam: Flare wedge opening upward, 40 degrees, behind a product screen. Once per page at most, never on the same page as a marker block.

## 6. Layout
- One Flare sentence and one primary button per view.
- Reading text only on Paper or White. On Ink: headline, figure, one line.
- Ink stage only for the opening and the close.
- Square corners. No rounded cards, no pills. Two exceptions: phone frames, and portraits of Mehmet. Portraits are always circles, echoing the ring (opening: inside the ring; What you get with me: 400 px desktop, 200 px mobile). No shadows, gradients or decorative icons.
- Groups open with a 2 px Ink rule, inner dividers 1 px Rule.
- 12 columns, 64 px side margin desktop, 20 px mobile, text max 62 characters.
- Page rhythm: Ink opening, Paper offer, White proof, Paper story, Ink next step.
- Components: service row (dial + name + text + audience), proof figures, cut button + underlined text link, source tag [12], signed Expert note, story card, work tile.
- Story card (case pages, About): White card with a 2 px Ink rule on top, a mono label with its number, the first sentence set large in Epilogue 700, the rest as body text. Two or three cards sit side by side on desktop and stack on mobile. Long text never runs as one block.
- Work tile (service pages, What we do together): White tile on Paper, three across on desktop. A small dial fills the slice for that step, then the step name in Epilogue 800 and one line in Stone.
- Pair and result: two story cards side by side (the situation and what we did), then one wider result card under them with a 4 px Ink rule, a full dial and a larger first sentence. Used on case pages and About (Who I am, Why I do this, then How I work). Bulleted lists use small Ink squares, never numbers.
- Spark uses no slices and only one Flare touch per view. Formats are numbered (01 The Last Day, 02 Sector reports). The number is solid when the format is live and an outline when it is in preparation. Issues sit underneath their format and carry their own number (Nº 01). A format page carries a Spark strip under the header with the current format underlined, and a large outline number in its opening.
- Clickable rows: a row that opens a page ends with a text ("Read the case", "Read") and a square Ink button with a Paper arrow (64 px desktop, 44 px mobile on Work; 40 to 48 px in lists). Rows that do not open anything have no arrow.
- Lists that grow (The Last Day episodes): one compact row per item, newest first, ten rows then "Show all". Spark shows at most three issues per format.
- Back link: every subpage opens with a small mono link back one level (← Home, ← Work, ← Spark), 44 px tall.

## 6b. Motion
- One easing everywhere: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1). Durations 160, 240 and 480 ms.
- Things that load move once per page view. Nothing loops except the Spark ticker, which pauses on hover.
- The ninth slice arrives last and stays still.
- Under prefers-reduced-motion every movement is instant and the ticker stops.

## 7. Voice
- Mehmet speaks (site first screen, LinkedIn, first email): first person, short, B&W portrait. "I've built two digital banks. I'll tell you what breaks first." Approved lines: "I lead every project. A small delivery team builds with me." · "Ürün canlıya çıkana kadar ekibinizle çalışırım."
- fspark9 speaks (reports, proposals, Spark): sourced, plain, ends with a clear view ("Our view" / "Görüşümüz"). Personal voice only via signed Expert note / Uzman notu.
- Rules: one idea per sentence. No dashes of any kind ("interest free", "30 minute call"). No "X, not Y" / "X değil, Y". No filler (leverage, seamless, unlock, ecosystem, synergy, end to end, uçtan uca, sinerji, dönüşüm yolculuğu, değer yaratmak). Every figure sourced or marked as estimate. Turkish written as Turkish, conversational. fspark9 always lowercase.
- Formats: EN 40,000 / 4.92 / €2.5m / September 2026. TR 40.000 / 4,92 / 2,5 milyon € / Eylül 2026.
